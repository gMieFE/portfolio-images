Privacy Policy

App: ND2_Goda_Miezyte (package lt.vilniustech.gsk.mkdf_23_2.GodaM)
Developer: Goda Miežytė
Effective date: 25 November 2026

This app is a personal portfolio and CV app. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it. In short: the app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. Everything the app saves stays on your device.

1. Information stored only on your device

  • Profile photo. If you choose “Change photo”, the app opens your camera app. The photo you take is saved in the app’s private storage on your device and is shown on the home screen. The app does not upload it anywhere.
  • CV entries and projects you add. Text you add with “+ Add entry” or “+ Add project” is saved in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • Language choice. The app remembers whether you chose English or Lithuanian.

The developer cannot see or access any of this information. If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include the app’s data in your own device backup (for example, in your Google account). That backup is controlled by you and by Google, not by the developer.

2. Contact form

The “Contact me” form does not send anything by itself. When you press “Send”, the app opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent unless you send it from your email app. If you do, the developer receives your email like any other email and uses it only to reply to you.

3. Internet use

The app uses the internet only to download portfolio images from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). As with any website request, GitHub may receive technical information such as your IP address; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Links in the app (for example, to GitHub projects) open in your browser, where that website’s own privacy policy applies.

4. What the app does not do

  • No user accounts or logins.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No location, contacts or other personal data is accessed.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete everything the app has saved by uninstalling the app or by clearing its data in Android Settings → Apps → ND2_Goda_Miezyte → Storage → Clear data. CV entries and projects can also be deleted inside the app.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: miezyte.goda@gmail.com
